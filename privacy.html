Privacy Policy

Kerfloop ("the game") is made by Woolyback LLC ("we", "us").

Effective date: 26 November 2026

The short version

The game does not collect, store on our servers, use or share any personal information or any other data about you or your child. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no internet features.

What the game keeps, and where

The game saves a few things on your device only, so it works the way you left it:

  • machines you build, in up to 20 save slots, including any names typed for them
  • which challenges have been completed
  • settings, such as music and sound on or off, and whether the first-time tour has been seen

This information never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for a name, email address, age, location, photos, contacts or any other personal information.
  • It does not use advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party data collection tools.
  • It does not connect to the internet or contain links out of the game.
  • It does not offer accounts, chat or sharing with other people.

Children

The game is made for children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

App stores and your device

You get the game from an app store such as the Apple App Store, Google Play or Steam. That store handles your purchase and account under its own privacy policy, not this one. If you have chosen to share diagnostics with Apple or Google, they may make anonymous crash reports about the game available to us. We use them only to fix problems.

Keeping and deleting data

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete. Everything the game saves stays on your device. To delete it, uninstall the game.

Security

Because nothing is sent from the game, no data about you is transferred or stored by us. Saved machines are protected by your device's own security.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it here and change the effective date above. If the game ever collects any data in future, we will update this policy before that happens.
